What is Fileport?

Fileport connects your phone and computer on the same Wi-Fi network for direct file transfer and chat.

Files are encrypted with FPTP/1. Transfer history stays on your device.

Waiting for your computer

Start Fileport setup on your computer. Keep both devices on the same Wi-Fi / LAN network.

The computer will find this phone automatically. The six-digit passkey will appear here when pairing begins.
